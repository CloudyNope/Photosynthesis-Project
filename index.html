🌱 Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis is how plants make their own food using sunlight. It's basically how almost all life on Earth gets its energy!

6CO₂ + 6H₂O + light energy → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂

What is Photosynthesis?

Photosynthesis is the process plants use to turn sunlight into energy. They take in carbon dioxide from the air and water from the soil, and use energy from the sun to turn those things into glucose. During this process they release oxygen.

This all happens inside tiny structures in plant cells called chloroplasts. They contain a green pigment called chlorophyll, which is what makes plants green and what actually captures the sunlight.

Without photosynthesis, no creature would survive, as the oxygen plants release is what keeps us alive.

Key Vocabulary

These are the important words you need to know for photosynthesis 👇

Photosynthesis
The process plants use to make food (sugar) from sunlight, water, and CO₂.
Chloroplast
The part of a plant cell where photosynthesis happens. Like the "kitchen" of the cell.
Chlorophyll
The green pigment inside chloroplasts that soaks up sunlight which is why plants are green
Thylakoid
Flat, pancake-like sacs inside chloroplasts where the light reactions happen.
Stroma
The liquid that fills the chloroplast around the thylakoids. This is where the Calvin Cycle happens.
ATP
The cell's energy currency. Made during the light reactions and used to power the Calvin Cycle.
NADPH
A molecule that carries electrons and energy. Helps build glucose in the Calvin Cycle.
Calvin Cycle
The second stage of photosynthesis where CO₂ gets turned into glucose using ATP and NADPH.
Stomata
Tiny holes on the bottom of leaves where CO₂ comes in and O₂ goes out. Almost like our noses.
Glucose
The sugar that plants make during photosynthesis. It's their food and energy source.
Photolysis
When light energy splits water molecules apart. This releases the oxygen we breathe.
Carbon Fixation
When CO₂ from the air gets "fixed" into solid sugar molecules the plant can use.

The 3 Things Plants Need

For photosynthesis to work, plants need three main ingredients. Here's what each one does:

☀️ Sunlight

Sunlight is the energy that powers everything. Chlorophyll soaks it up and uses it to make ATP (energy).

  • Absorbed by chlorophyll
  • Powers the light reactions
  • Splits water molecules
  • Makes ATP and NADPH
Energy Source

💧 Water (H₂O)

Water gets absorbed through the roots and travels up to the leaves. When light hits it, it splits apart and releases oxygen!

  • Absorbed through roots
  • Gets split by light energy (photolysis)
  • Releases oxygen as a by-product
  • Provides electrons for the process
Electron Donor

🌬️ Carbon Dioxide (CO₂)

CO₂ floats in from the air through tiny holes in leaves called stomata. The plant grabs it and turns it into sugar.

  • Enters through stomata in leaves
  • Gets "fixed" by RuBisCO enzyme
  • Combined with other molecules
  • Eventually becomes glucose
Carbon Source

The 2 Stages of Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis happens in two steps. The first step needs light, and the second step just uses the energy made in step 1.

Stage 1 — Light Reactions ☀️

This happens in the thylakoid membranes. The plant captures sunlight and uses it to make energy molecules (ATP and NADPH). Water gets split apart and oxygen is released.

  • Chlorophyll absorbs sunlight
  • Water is split (photolysis) → releases O₂
  • Electrons move through a chain
  • ATP and NADPH are produced
  • Oxygen floats out through stomata

Stage 2 — Calvin Cycle 🔄

This happens in the stroma. The plant uses the ATP and NADPH from Stage 1 to turn CO₂ into glucose. No light is directly needed for this stage.

  • CO₂ is grabbed from the air
  • RuBisCO enzyme helps fix the carbon
  • ATP and NADPH are used up
  • G3P molecules are made
  • G3P gets turned into glucose

The Chemical Equation

Here's the full equation for photosynthesis. Six CO₂ molecules and six water molecules combine with light energy to make one glucose molecule and six oxygen molecules.

6 CO₂ + 6 H₂O + light energy → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6 O₂
carbon dioxide water → glucose (sugar) oxygen

Why Does Photosynthesis Matter?

Photosynthesis is way more important than just keeping plants alive. Here's why it matters for everyone:

🌬️

Oxygen

Almost all the oxygen we breathe comes from photosynthesis. We would die without it.

🍎

Food

Plants are at the bottom of pretty much every food chain. Everything we eat traces back to them.

🌡️

Climate

Plants absorb CO₂ from the air, helping to slow down climate change.

⛽

Fossil Fuels

Coal and oil are made from ancient plants. They're basically stored sunlight from millions of years ago.
